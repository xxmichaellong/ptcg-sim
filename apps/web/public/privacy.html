Privacy

PTCG Sim · Updated November 10, 2026

You can play PTCG Sim without an account. Decks and settings then stay in your browser on this device, and we keep nothing about you.

When you sign in

Signing in with Google or Discord keeps:

  • the name and avatar you choose;
  • your sign-in methods: the provider's id for you, and the email address (Google) or username (Discord) that shows which account it is;
  • when you joined;
  • which browsers are signed in, as random codes with the date each was last used. A browser unused for 90 days is signed out.

We never see your Google or Discord password, and we keep none of their tokens: we ask who you are once, when you sign in.

As account features arrive, your account also keeps what you save to it: decks, settings, saved games and replays.

Cookies

One cookie keeps you signed in. Another lasts ten minutes while you sign in. There are no ads, no tracking cookies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing.

Where it's kept

On Cloudflare, which runs PTCG Sim. Games in progress are kept on its servers while you play, and removed after they go unused.

Children

Accounts are for players 13 and older. If we learn an account belongs to someone younger, we delete it. Anyone can still play without one.

Deleting your account

In Settings, open your account and choose Delete account. Everything it keeps is deleted straight away and can't be brought back. Decks in your browser stay there.

Questions

Use Report a bug in Settings, or open an issue on GitHub.
